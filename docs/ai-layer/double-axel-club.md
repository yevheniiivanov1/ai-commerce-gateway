## Program

Level 3 of the VSA jump ladder. For skaters who are already proficient in double jumps off-ice and want to make the transition to the Double Axel off-ice, and to prepare for triple jumps. Classes are live and interactive and run from home; the program progresses from technique fundamentals through power, rotational speed and harder drills over six months.

- **Technique development:** Correct approach and setup for a successful jump; Generating the rotation and height the Double Axel needs; Precise landings for stability and lower injury risk
- **Power and explosiveness:** Plyometric training for takeoff strength; Bounding drills that mimic the Double Axel's explosive energy; Core strengthening to stabilise the rotation axis
- **Rotational speed:** Off-ice spins for rotational control and speed; Double-revolution drills to build muscle memory
- **Progression:** Starts with basic drills and increases difficulty as technique improves; Moves into more complex drills and introduces the transition to harder double jumps and triple jumps

**You need:** Sneakers, Jump rope, Off-ice spinner, Stable internet connection, Some space to jump.
